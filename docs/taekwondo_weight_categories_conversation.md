For the **2026 TFI National Open Taekwondo Championships in India**, the **Sub-Junior Kyorugi** weight categories are:

| No. | Boys / Male | Girls / Female |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Under 16 kg | Under 14 kg |
| 2 | Under 18 kg | Under 16 kg |
| 3 | Under 21 kg | Under 18 kg |
| 4 | Under 23 kg | Under 20 kg |
| 5 | Under 25 kg | Under 22 kg |
| 6 | Under 27 kg | Under 24 kg |
| 7 | Under 29 kg | Under 26 kg |
| 8 | Under 32 kg | Under 29 kg |
| 9 | Under 35 kg | Under 32 kg |
| 10 | Under 38 kg | Under 35 kg |
| 11 | Under 41 kg | Under 38 kg |
| 12 | Under 44 kg | Under 41 kg |
| 13 | Under 50 kg | Under 47 kg |
| 14 | Under 60 kg | Under 57 kg |

For example, **Boys Under 21 kg** means **over 18 kg and not exceeding 21 kg**. **Girls Under 29 kg** means **over 26 kg and not exceeding 29 kg**.

Source:  
https://www.scribd.com/document/1025711149/12-NOTC-RR-202603-OL-0

One important point for a Taekwondo scoring/tournament system: these are **TFI Sub-Junior categories**, not the same thing as WT Cadet categories. WT uses a separate **Cadet** classification.

## User
Tell me about senior weight categorys

## Assistant
For **World Taekwondo (WT) Senior Kyorugi**, there are **8 male and 8 female weight categories**.

| # | Senior Male | Senior Female |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Under 54 kg | Under 46 kg |
| 2 | Under 58 kg | Under 49 kg |
| 3 | Under 63 kg | Under 53 kg |
| 4 | Under 68 kg | Under 57 kg |
| 5 | Under 74 kg | Under 62 kg |
| 6 | Under 80 kg | Under 67 kg |
| 7 | Under 87 kg | Under 73 kg |
| 8 | Over 87 kg | Over 73 kg |

The exact ranges work like this: **Male Under 63 kg = over 58 kg and not exceeding 63 kg**, while **Female Under 57 kg = over 53 kg and not exceeding 57 kg**.

WT Competition Rules source:  
https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/att_file/documents/WT%20Competition%20Rules%20and%20Interpretation%20%28September%2030%2C%202024%29.pdf

One thing to keep separate in a tournament system: **Olympic senior divisions are different**—they use only **4 divisions per gender**:

- Male: −58 kg, −68 kg, −80 kg, +80 kg
- Female: −49 kg, −57 kg, −67 kg, +67 kg

Normal WT World-level senior competition uses the **8 divisions above**.
